## F100: `src/bike_sim/recording/encoding.py`

**Source:** lines 1-425; SHA-256 `766db8904bdc5e6fc46530e21b9e64886ed6329b3181e626a463bd848bca1685`. **Owner:** T39; **deletion:** T63 after row evidence.

**Source module purpose:** Typed batch encoding of reviewed rows (R08). Rows are ordinary Python data at documented reference values: * ``physics`` rows address fields by their source name (``time_s``, ``end_time_s``, ``qpos``, ``qvel``, ...); ``channels``/``forces``/ ``powers_w`` members are delegated to the other tables and exempted. * ``forces`` rows are exploded per component (see ``force_rows``). * channel-table rows carry their claimed ``channels`` roots at top level: ``{'energy': {...}, 'component_work_j': {...}}``. A root member is routed to the field whose ``path`` matches; a ``map<*>``/``struct`` field placed at the root itself receives the remaining members verbatim. ``encode_rows`` materializes a ``TableBatch`` fragment against a ``TableSpec``; ``decode_rows`` rebuilds rows for tests. Optional fields project to ``<key>_present`` categories plus a value column so absence is recorded explicitly rather than inferred from a padding value. Doubles are preserved verbatim — NaN and ±inf round-trip and never
