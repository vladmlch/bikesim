# Task A5 report

## Change

- Physical `CoilShock` clamps coil compression at zero; `legacy_behavior=True` keeps the old signed linear law. The ride factory selects the law from `physics_config` even when coil specs are supplied by the caller. Invalid preload, rate, stroke, bumper length and peak force are rejected.
- Added a unilateral `end_stop` force and potential calculator. The standalone top-out example returns 450 N and 0.4 J. The physical shock uses a lower top-out before zero stroke and transfers the quadratic bumper's 7000 N and 23.333333 J at full working stroke to a preloaded upper emergency stop on overtravel. At the exact working-stroke boundary the bumper owns the force and energy; above it the upper stop owns them. Contact damping is passive and may engage after penetration.
- `EndStopConfig` is frozen, marked `synthetic`, and shared by MJCF and the force path. Defaults are 7000 N at 10 mm, 500 N s/m damping, 700000 N/m derived stiffness, and 10 mm overtravel at each end. It rejects overtravel shorter than the reference deflection so the solver limit cannot intervene before that calibration point. Physical `shock_stroke` range is `[-0.010, stroke+0.010]` m; legacy range is unchanged. The joint validator permits a negative lower range only for the physical shock, while the fork still requires zero.
- Physical steps register separate `fork_spring`, `fork_damper`, `shock_coil`, `shock_bumper`, `shock_damper`, `shock_top_out`, and `shock_upper_stop` generalized force vectors. `SuspensionForceApplier.potential_energy_j` exposes named coil, bumper and stop energies. Aggregate `compute_qfrc` and direct `apply` remain available.

## Review fix round 1 — stroke ownership and native solver limit

- `RideSimulation` now rejects an explicitly injected coil whose `stroke_mm` differs from active `BikeSpecs.shock_stroke` before MJCF compilation; the error shows both values. Without an injected coil, it creates `CoilShockSpecs` from active bike stroke and spring rate. A supplied `CoilShock` instance is passed through unchanged, including subclass overrides and its existing `legacy_behavior` flag. The internally created default alone follows the selected legacy/physical mode.
- Added `shock_joint_limit_qfrc`, which selects only MuJoCo `mjCNSTR_LIMIT_JOINT` EFC rows with the `shock_stroke` joint ID and maps their multipliers with `mj_mulJacTVec`. Contact rows can share the numeric EFC ID but are excluded by type. A separate immutable `last_constraint_snapshot` is captured after `mj_step` and cleared on reset; the pre-step `ForceSample.components` remains unchanged. V2 recording integrates the named `shock_solver_limit` power and work for that completed interval, using its incoming velocity, and rejects a snapshot from a different interval. This is a sampled work estimate, not a closed global energy residual.
