## F170: `src/bike_sim/sim/ride/input.py`

**Source:** lines 1-324; SHA-256 `8b55c9e56f05d59f21af32da89b993fea49855d540389ef83d82dc47c613ed97`. **Owner:** T49; **deletion:** T63 after row evidence.

**Source module purpose:** Ride-Mode Input & Key Event Dispatcher. Maps GLFW keycodes to ride-session actions through one declarative lookup table, in the same shape as `sim/input_handler.py` does for the test stand. **Every damper and air-spring key keeps its playground binding**, deliberately: the two modes adjust the same two hardware models, and a rider who has learnt `J`/`H` on the stand should not have to relearn it on the track. The ride-only keys are `W`/`S` for cruise, `Space` for the brakes, `,`/`.` for brake strength, and `N`/`M`/`;`/`'` for pneumatic tyre pressure.
