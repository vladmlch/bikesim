## F103: `src/bike_sim/recording/native.py`

**Source:** lines 1-370; SHA-256 `27def9d2e5e8a380b729c54e902cd30e9adfbe6990dc3b260ef7ca0d636eff51`. **Owner:** T40; **deletion:** T63 after row evidence.

**Source module purpose:** Catalog-governed typed encoding of a sealed native episode (A2). ``NativePhysicalSource`` adapts an ``OwnedNativeEpisode`` — or any duck-typed value exposing ``generation``, ``row_count`` and ``batch(start, limit)`` — to the ``PhysicalSource`` contract. Every table in the bound catalog is emitted through the reviewed ``encode_rows`` encoder. Each bounded native sample batch is projected once into all domain tables; unknown channel roots reject their batch, and absent optional roots encode explicit NULLs rather than padding. ``iter_derived_events`` re-reads a sealed episode and emits compact ``derived_transition`` input-journal events where the instrumented channels change. These are DERIVED facts over retained rows — labeled with their derivation rule and version, never source events — so the journal keeps controller gate flips readable without a second per-row recording pass.

**Source module purpose:** Ride-Mode Telemetry Recorder. Collects one row of channels per recorded step from a :class:`RideSimulation` and writes them as CSV with the standard library. Channel conventions: - Positions and travel in metres / millimetres as named; velocities in m/s; forces in N. - ``*_contact`` flags are 0 / 1 as the contact query reports them (debounced). - Accelerometer channels are **proper acceleration** resolved into world axes: at rest the vertical channel reads +9.81 m/s^2, in free fall 0. ``vert`` is world Z, ``long`` world X. The raw solver transients on sharp edges are recorded as they are; filtering is the summary's job, not the recorder's. - The virtual rider's moment, accumulated angular impulse and accumulated work are recorded so an energy audit can account for what the rider injected. - The seated rider's interface loads (saddle, front and rear pedal, bar) are the forces
